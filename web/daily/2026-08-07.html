Instacart's Blueberry assistant combines AI agents with operational data and historical incident knowledge to help on-call engineers investigate production issues faster.

Playbook takeaway

ProblemOn-call engineers waste the first, most time-critical minutes of an incident manually pulling logs, deploys, and past-incident context before they can even start diagnosing.

ApplyOn alert, fan out parallel read-only subagents that pull logs, deployments, service ownership, and historical incident data through MCP tools, ground each root-cause hypothesis against past incidents to cut noise, and keep the system advisory-only — post the hypothesis to the incident thread rather than auto-remediating.

ExpectedInstacart's Blueberry delivers a grounded root-cause hypothesis within 3 minutes across 270+ Slack channels, with historical grounding lifting diagnostic accuracy from the mid-60% range to the high-90% range.
