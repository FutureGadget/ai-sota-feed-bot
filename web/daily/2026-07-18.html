Controlling Reasoning Effort in LLMs

sebastian_raschkaJul 18Details

Sebastian Raschka catalogs how models implement low/medium/high reasoning modes: GPT-5.6's system-prompt conditioning, RL length penalties, Qwen3's mixed thinking/non-thinking fine-tuning, Kimi K2.5's alternating-phase toggle, and DeepSeek V4's specialist distillation.

Playbook takeaway

ProblemRunning every agent call at maximum reasoning effort burns tokens on steps where extra reasoning barely moves accuracy.

ApplySet an explicit low/medium/high reasoning-effort level per call type instead of one global setting — reserve high effort for tasks where the accuracy gap over medium is real, and default routine tool-calling or classification steps to medium or low.

ExpectedThe piece reports medium effort typically holds 85-95% of max-effort benchmark performance at 50-70% of the token cost, and that Kimi K2.5's effort toggle cut tokens 25-30% with little accuracy change.
