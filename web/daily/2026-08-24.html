Building a 100x Cheaper Trace Judge with Fireworks

langchain_blogDetails

LangChain and Fireworks fine-tuned an open model to catch production trace errors, matching frontier-model judge accuracy at roughly 1/100th the inference cost.

Playbook takeaway

ProblemRunning a frontier model as an LLM judge over every production trace to catch agent errors gets expensive fast at scale.

ApplyFine-tune a smaller open model on your own perceived-error labels, as LangChain and Fireworks did, and use it as the trace judge instead of calling a frontier model on every trace.

ExpectedLangChain and Fireworks report the fine-tuned judge matches frontier-model judgment quality at roughly 100x lower cost per trace.
