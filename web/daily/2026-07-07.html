Improving Agents is a Data Mining Problem

langchain_blogDetails

LangChain argues agent improvement is fundamentally about mining trace data, fine-tuning small open judge models that beat frontier LLMs on narrow eval tasks for a fraction of the cost — one harness change delivered a 13.7% lift on Terminal-Bench 2.0.

Playbook takeaway

ProblemReading agent traces one by one to find failure patterns doesn't scale, and running a frontier LLM as a judge on every eval gets expensive fast once you're iterating constantly.

ApplyRun specialized agents over your full trace history to mine for specific failure signals, use what they find to curate targeted training data, then fine-tune a small open judge model on that narrow task instead of defaulting to a frontier-model judge. Use the fine-tuned judge's scores to hill-climb agent performance in your eval loop.

ExpectedLangChain reports that on narrow judge tasks, fine-tuned open models matched or beat frontier judges while running orders of magnitude cheaper, trading token cost for a one-time fine-tuning and infrastructure cost.
