Evaluating AI Agents: A production blueprint with Strands and AgentCore

aws_ml_blogDetails

Motorway and AWS built an eval pipeline on Strands Agents and AgentCore that cut wrong results from 1 in 8 queries to 1 in 50 and slashed incident-detection time from hours to minutes.

Playbook takeaway

ProblemMotorway's dealer-search agent shipped with no way to catch tool-selection errors or multi-turn context drift before dealers saw them.

ApplyScore every candidate release on three thresholds — tool usage >95%, reasoning >85%, output quality >90% — using strands-agents-evals pre-deploy and Bedrock AgentCore Evaluations in production, and block deployment in a five-stage pipeline when any threshold misses. For non-deterministic runs, track pass^k (success across k consecutive trials) instead of a single pass/fail.

ExpectedTool selection accuracy rose from 87% to 98%, task completion from 82% to 96%, and production incidents fell from 12/month to 2 — cutting incorrect results from 1 in 8 queries to 1 in 50.
