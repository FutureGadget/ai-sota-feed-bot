Agent Security and Data Exposure 3 items

Leaks and hijacks show up in the channels output-only audits never read: inter-agent messages, tool-call text and a third-party inference vendor.

A 1,000-scenario benchmark across 7 channels and 5 LLMs finds inter-agent messages leak sensitive data 68.8% of the time against 27.2% for final outputs; output-only audits miss 41.7%.

Google tested its trace-reading detector for agent goal hijack, OWASP's top agentic risk for 2026, against third-party benchmarks; it runs asynchronously on traces and logs, adding no latency.
