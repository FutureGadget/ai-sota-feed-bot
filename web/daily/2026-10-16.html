Playbook takeaway

ProblemTeams have no shared, quantitative answer to whether an agent is behaving well in production.

ApplyDefine one or two agent-behavior SLIs (for example task success rate on sampled traces), set an SLO target, and track burn against an error budget as you do for services.

ExpectedBehavior regressions page someone through the same reliability process as outages, instead of surfacing as anecdotes.
