Grab Cuts Mechanical Analytics Work From 44% to 30% with AI Agents

infoq_ai_mlDetails

Grab's AI agents cut mechanical analytics work from 44% of analyst time in February to 30% by June, combining agent autonomy with certified data and human oversight.

Playbook takeaway

ProblemHanding an agent full autonomy on messy analytics work either stalls adoption from excess caution or breaks trust the first time it gets something wrong.

ApplyDefine explicit autonomy levels tied to risk: at a mid level, the agent discovers data, writes queries, and drafts analysis while a human frames the question and reviews the output; at a higher level it plans and orchestrates a multi-step workflow behind a human gate review. Route each request to the matching level, and keep humans owning metric definitions, causal interpretation, and final decisions at every level.

ExpectedGrab cut mechanical analyst tickets from 44% to 30% in five months while self-service SQL requests rose from 50% to 81%, without ceding decision authority.
