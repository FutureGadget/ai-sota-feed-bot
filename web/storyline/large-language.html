Builder action

Before trusting RAG citations, sample answers and check that the cited passage actually supports the claim; when a regression eval moves, re-score an older baseline with today's judges or rubric first to rule out rater drift.

Earlier contextThe story so far

This thread was regrouped from a speculative-decoding run into a broad bucket of 77 papers and posts sharing the phrase "large language", mostly arXiv, from Nov 6. Its largest strands are hallucination fixes, which moved from preference alignment to decode-time methods, eval-validity papers, and RAG and agent-memory designs.
