Follow in this browser to see new updates on your Live feed.

Current stateDomestic-chip identity confirmed, scaling for overseas hostingstatus changed Sep 1 · no fixed release date for the original GLM-5.3 or named overseas provider yet

Latest change

Z.ai's domestic-chip inference build — the same stack behind the since-unmasked "Ox Alpha"/GLM-5.3-Flash model — has scaled to 100,000 Chinese AI chips, with the company reportedly courting overseas cloud providers to host it.

Builder action

Before trusting GLM-5.3-Flash's 1/40-of-Opus pricing or cyber-test scores, rerun them against your own eval suite — independent pieces have already contested both the benchmark headline and the domestic-chip framing, and no overseas hosting provider or capacity commitment is confirmed yet.

Earlier contextThe story so far

GLM-5.3 launched claiming top open-weights coding results, then slipped past its expected release after Z.ai disclosed a cybersecurity risk. Z.ai then revealed the buzzy, unbranded "Ox Alpha" model was actually GLM-5.3-Flash — running on 100,000 domestic Chinese chips with no Nvidia hardware, at roughly 1/40 Opus 4.8's price.
