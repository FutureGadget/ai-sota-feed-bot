Fable Mythos

On Bedrock · data-sharing requiredstatus changed Jun 20 · available via provider_data_share opt-in

Fable 5 and Mythos 5 launched "made safe for general use," were pulled by a US export-control directive, then returned on Amazon Bedrock — but only with provider_data_share, sending prompts and outputs to Anthropic for 30-day retention with human review.

TL;DR

Anthropic launched Claude Fable 5 and the higher Mythos 5 tier on Jun 9, billing the Mythos-class model as made safe for general use but shipping it with controversial usage terms. The first sustained hands-on read Fable 5 as "relentlessly proactive." Then on Jun 12-13 the story turned: a US government export-control directive, citing national security, suspended access to Fable 5 and Mythos 5, and coverage recast the launch as models too dangerous to release. A Jun 15 recap framed the takedown as a temporary suspension. By Jun 20 access is back on Amazon Bedrock — but only by opting into provider_data_share, sending prompts and outputs to Anthropic for 30-day retention with human review.

What's new: Access is partially restored: Fable 5 and Mythos 5 are usable on Amazon Bedrock again, but require opting into provider_data_share — prompts and outputs go to Anthropic for 30-day retention with human review, a break from how previous Bedrock models kept inference data private.
