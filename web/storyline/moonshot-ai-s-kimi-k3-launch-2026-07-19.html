Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 launch

Follow in this browser to see new updates on your Live feed.

Current stateBenchmark-leading, IP dispute unresolvedstatus changed Aug 9

Latest change

Kimi K3 became the first Chinese model to top a major coding benchmark on Aug 9, capping a week where Apple wired rival Alibaba's Qwen into macOS Siri for China and Alibaba began charging its biggest Qwen users.

Builder action

If you're evaluating open-weight coding models, benchmark Kimi K3's 2.8T MoE (104B active params, 1M-token context, Kimi Delta Attention) against your own eval suite now, not the marketing benchmark — self-hosting needs serious GPU capacity, and the unresolved IP-provenance dispute means you shouldn't assume redistribution or fine-tuning rights are clean.

Earlier contextThe story so far

Moonshot AI's July 20 launch of Kimi K3 — a 2.8T open-weight model — jolted Bitcoin and chip stocks and drew a Trump administration accusation that it was distilled from Anthropic's tech via banned Nvidia chips. Moonshot answered by open-sourcing the weights July 27, pushing a $50B Hong Kong IPO, and landing early adoption from Microsoft and Cursor.
