Earlier contextThe story so far

DeepSeek tied its V4.1-Flash model to its own agent harness on Oct 17, then on Oct 20 open-sourced DeepInfer, the inference engine behind its production services. On Oct 31 it published the architecture of an always-on coding agent, dsh-superagent.
