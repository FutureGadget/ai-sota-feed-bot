Follow in this browser to see new updates on your Live feed.

Current stateShipped · V4-Pro traffic silently rerouted, now on third-party hosts toostatus changed Sep 14

Latest change

Third-party host ZenMux added DeepSeek V4.1-Flash for high-throughput reasoning on Sep 22, the first sign the model is spreading to inference platforms beyond DeepSeek's own API.

Builder action

Re-run your eval suite against the V4-Pro endpoint: you're getting V4.1-Flash's outputs at a still-falling price, and any prompt or behavior tuned to V4-Pro specifically may have silently drifted. If you route through ZenMux or another third-party host, verify it serves the same weights before trusting cross-provider evals.

Earlier contextThe story so far

DeepSeek shipped V4.1-Flash on Sep 10, claiming it beats flagship V4-Pro while cutting the memory agents need to run it. The efficiency claim rattled Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron shares within a day, and DeepSeek quantified it Sep 13 as a 75% HBM and 87.5% SSD cut.
