Deep Research

Active researchstatus changed Jun 18

A fast-moving research thread where the success criterion for deep research agents keeps being redefined — evaluation, workflow prediction, and now report-generation structure.

TL;DR

Through mid-June, deep research agents kept getting redefined faster than they could be standardized. An arXiv paper argued that benchmarks only grade a single-shot report and miss whether an agent improves under feedback, and ran a multi-turn process-level evaluation. AWS then shipped a build pattern: an end-to-end competitive research agent on Deep Agents and Bedrock AgentCore with isolated multi-step execution. DRFLOW moved the target toward predicting a personalized, enterprise-style workflow rather than just a report, and the latest beat, ScaffoldAgent, attacks the long-form report itself — utility-guided dynamic outline optimization for open-ended deep research.

What's new: The newest item, ScaffoldAgent (Jun 18), shifts the focus from what to evaluate back to how to generate: it frames open-ended deep research as multi-round retrieval feeding a coherent long-form report, and makes the outline the optimization target with a utility-guided, dynamically optimized scaffold — a generation-side answer after a run of benchmark-side papers.
