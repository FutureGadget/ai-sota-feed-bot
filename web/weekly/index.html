We're going to need default hard budget caps on pretty much everything

simon_willisonOct 3Details

Simon Willison argues pay-by-usage services need default hard budget caps as agents spend autonomously.

Playbook takeaway

ProblemA looping or runaway agent on pay-per-use APIs can burn an unbounded bill before anyone notices.

ApplyEnforce a hard per-run and per-day spend ceiling at the gateway or key level, so the call is refused at the cap instead of alerting after the fact.

ExpectedA runaway loop costs at most the cap, not the month's budget.

Cloudflare Uses an AI Harness to Probe and Harden Its WAF

infoq_ai_mlDetails

Cloudflare used frontier models in a controlled harness to probe and harden its WAF.

Playbook takeaway

ProblemStatic test suites for a protective layer go stale as soon as attackers vary their payloads.

ApplyRun models inside a sandboxed harness that takes blocked requests from your logs, has the model mutate them, and reports which variants get through.

ExpectedCloudflare reports this surfaces WAF bypasses from variants of attacks it already blocks, before attackers find them.
