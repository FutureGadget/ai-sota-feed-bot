The week in signals

  • Claude Fable 5.2 and Gemini 4 Carbon shipped one day apart.
  • Gemini Decisions API scores up to 1,000 yes/no and rubric questions in a single pass.
  • vLLM on one Vera Rubin NVL72 rack served 387 tokens/s per user at 58x GB300 throughput.
  • Roughly one in four of 100+ serverless endpoints scored below its reference deployment.
  • Agent memory leaked confidential data in 2 of 3 background tasks where raw RAG leaked none.
  • Backend skills cut critical mistakes from 25 of 150 runs to 2 of 150 under hidden checks.

Three launches landed in three days: Claude Fable 5.2 on November 9, Gemini 4 Carbon on November 10 and Google's Gemini Decisions API on November 11. The third changes the most for builders, because a calibrated yes/no model for routing and grading now sits beside the flagships as its own tier.

The layers beneath agents looked less trustworthy. Fireworks widened its October breach to account and billing data, Phala reported an attack on its confidential-AI service, and Artificial Analysis found roughly one in four hosted endpoints scoring below the reference weights.

The response was verification with evidence the agent cannot edit: hooks that catch weakened tests, hidden checks for skills, and a formal proof of Signal's protocol code. Check the endpoint, the provider and the diff yourself, because a model name or a green build no longer settles it.
