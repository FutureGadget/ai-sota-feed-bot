Kimi K3.1

Follow in this browser to see new updates on your Live feed.

Current stateOpen weights · ecosystem rolloutstatus changed Nov 5

Latest change

Huawei Ascend now supports LoRA fine-tuning of Kimi K3.1 Pro on a 128-card Atlas 950 SuperPoD, following Moore Threads day-zero serving support and the Nov 5 open-weights release.

Builder action

Read the weights license commercial-use conditions before planning self-hosting, budget for a large multi-GPU cluster given the 1.56TB checkpoint, and keep a fallback model since the hosted API had quota problems at launch.

Earlier contextThe story so far

Moonshot AI's Kimi K3.1 Pro surfaced after Arena testing on Oct 23 and launched Oct 24 as a multi-trillion-parameter flagship served via API. A botched launch forced a quota reset and apology, and third-party hosts started listing it within days.
