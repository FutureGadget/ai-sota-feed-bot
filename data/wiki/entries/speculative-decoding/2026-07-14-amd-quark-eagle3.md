AMD's Quark toolkit now trains, quantizes, and serves **EAGLE-3** draft models with vLLM on Instinct GPUs. AMD reports up to **2.00x throughput for Kimi-K2.5** and 1.79x for MiniMax-M2.5.

Draft-and-verify is no longer tied to one vendor's silicon. Teams running mixed or non-NVIDIA fleets can expect the same serving knob to exist on both, though the draft model still has to be trained for each target.
