NVIDIA reports **up to 15x** inference-performance gains on Blackwell GPUs using DFlash, a speculative-decoding method that drafts several tokens in parallel rather than one at a time.

Treat the number as a vendor-reported ceiling, not a planning figure. The useful signal is that accelerator vendors now tune draft-and-verify for their own hardware instead of leaving it to the serving framework.
