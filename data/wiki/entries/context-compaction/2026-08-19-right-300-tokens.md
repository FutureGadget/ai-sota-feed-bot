Baruch Sadogursky and Patrick Debois argue coding agents fail from **bloated, stuffed context** more often than from a missing capability. Their fixes alongside summarization:

- lazy-loaded skills, whose instructions load only when the task needs them
- versioned context artifacts
- an externalized memory bank
- LLM-as-judge evals to catch quality the curation step loses

Compaction is one tool inside a curation discipline, not the whole answer.
