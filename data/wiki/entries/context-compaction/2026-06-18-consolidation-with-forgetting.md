An open-source memory plugin for OpenClaw agents replaces the built-in memory-core with a biologically inspired design that models **association, forgetting, and synthesis**. It builds more complex memories out of the initial facts it records, and consolidation deliberately forgets as part of that process.

The point for builders: consolidation is an active curation step, not a side effect of running out of space. What an agent drops matters as much as what it keeps.
