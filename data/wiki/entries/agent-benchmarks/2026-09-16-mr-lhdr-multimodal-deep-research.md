**Mr.LHDR** builds each question from a hidden node-relation graph requiring an average of **12.1 intermediate conclusions** at dependency depth 10.4, with at least one image, map, PDF, chart, table, or video frame that changes the reasoning.

It scores both the final answer and intermediate conclusions. The strongest system reaches **43.1% Overall and 34.3% Strict Accuracy**, so final-answer accuracy overstates research success. Removing images cuts the dependency-aware score by 12.6 points.
