**OmniaBench** derives an application-oriented taxonomy from app stores, product docs, and web retrieval, spanning 1,431 tasks across 90 top-level domains with explicit state spaces.

Even **frontier models clear only about half** the suite, headroom that narrower coding and tool-use benchmarks don't surface.
