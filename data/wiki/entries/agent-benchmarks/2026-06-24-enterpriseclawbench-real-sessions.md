EnterpriseClawBench mines **852 reproducible tasks** from an archive of real enterprise agent sessions where agents read heterogeneous files, call tools, and deliver business artifacts. Each task pairs recovered fixtures with rewritten prompts, role classes, hard rules, and semantic rubrics.

The benchmark inherits the messiness of production instead of approximating it. The catch: the sessions contain internal content, so the data is not released.
