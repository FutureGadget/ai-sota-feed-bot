**TREK** stress-tests trip-planning agents on a property single-answer benchmarks skip: every flight, hotel, and attraction must exist and be bookable, the days must be traversable, and the total must clear a budget, all at once. One wrong leg fails the whole plan.

It replaces soft or LLM-judged rubrics with checks that certify a plan is executable, reproducible, and auditable.
