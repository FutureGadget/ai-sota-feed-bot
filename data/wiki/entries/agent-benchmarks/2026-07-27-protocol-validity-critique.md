title: "Agent benchmark scores may reflect protocol artifacts, not capability"
date: 2026-07-27
theme: trusting-the-score
evidence: [ddce7e0a20f47f4f]
---
A protocol-validity analysis argues that many agent benchmarks **conflate task difficulty with scaffolding and protocol artifacts**. Two agents can score differently because of how their harness interacts with the benchmark's protocol, not because one is more capable.

This turns "the harness is part of what you benchmark" into a challenge to the benchmark's validity as a measuring instrument, not only its reproducibility.
