**MirrorCode** is a long-horizon coding benchmark where agents reimplement entire software projects by replicating an existing program's functionality. It targets the gap between short-task benchmarks and one-off demos (such as an AI-built C compiler) that are hard to compare because they involve human guidance and aren't repeated across models.

It moves the frontier from "modify existing code" to "reconstruct it from how it behaves."
