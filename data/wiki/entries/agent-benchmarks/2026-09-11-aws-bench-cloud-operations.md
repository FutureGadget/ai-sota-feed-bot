**AWS-Bench** scores coding agents on real AWS work, split into read-only introspection (diagnosing a live misconfiguration) and mutation (provisioning or modifying resources, checked programmatically against the resulting account state). Curated sets run from a 9-task quickstart to 78 "basic" and 47 "advanced" tasks, with reference pairings including Claude Sonnet 5.
