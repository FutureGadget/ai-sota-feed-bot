Two datasets (AIOps2025 and RCA100) evaluate LLM agents on **microservice failure diagnosis** over multimodal observability data. Grading covers the reasoning process along three dimensions, starting with localization (where the fault is) and identification (what type), rather than only the final answer.

It brings process-over-outcome grading to ops agents and pulls benchmarking toward [observability](/topic/agent-observability).
