**DPBench** is a structural benchmark for multi-agent LLM coordination: instead of one end-to-end score, it probes which structural factors determine whether agents coordinate successfully.

Isolating one failure axis tells you *why* a multi-agent setup underperforms, which an aggregate task score cannot.
