**HalluTruthQA** has 2,400 expert-curated Arabic QA examples across four domains (Islamic knowledge, history, science, geography). Each answer has a verified reference and six verification candidates; hallucinated answers carry character-level error spans and human-written explanations.

Zero-shot, **no open-source model tops every sub-task**. Grading is moving past English-centric, response-level hallucination labels toward non-English, finer-grained ones.
