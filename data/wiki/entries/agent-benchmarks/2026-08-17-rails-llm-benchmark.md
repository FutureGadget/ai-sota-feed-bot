The Rails Foundation commissioned the first systematic **Ruby on Rails** benchmark for coding agents, scoring accuracy, speed, token consumption, and cost across 8 frontier and open-weight models on self-contained tasks. It checks whether agents use **current Rails APIs** rather than stale training data.

A harder multi-step Stage 2 and an open-sourced Ruby harness are planned.
