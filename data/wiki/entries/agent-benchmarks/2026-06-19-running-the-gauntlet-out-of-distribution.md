"Running the Gauntlet" introduces **GauntletBench**, a web-based benchmark for agent generalization in challenging scenarios. It argues current benchmarks are built on popular applications with simple tasks, so modern agents saturate them without exposing their limits.

Agents that score well on familiar leaderboards **degrade sharply outside familiar environments**. A high public score is weak evidence for how an agent will do on your workload.
