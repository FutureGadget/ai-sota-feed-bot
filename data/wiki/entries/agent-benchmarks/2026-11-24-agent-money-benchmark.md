*Agent Money Benchmark* gives agents USDC on Solana and asks them to buy the information a task needs. Only **3 of 12** models passed **79 of 80** payment tasks, and estimated waste ranged from **$21.0 to $70.8 per $100** spent, through overpaying, duplicate purchases, or buying data the task did not need.

For builders: correct payment execution and sensible spending are separate skills. Put per-task budgets and duplicate-purchase checks outside the model before giving an agent a wallet.
