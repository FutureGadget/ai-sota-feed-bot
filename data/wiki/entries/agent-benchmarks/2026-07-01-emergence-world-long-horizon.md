Emergence AI built **Emergence World** specifically to evaluate long-horizon autonomy: sustained multi-step operation rather than a single bounded task.

Long horizons are the hardest distribution shift for an agent. A suite that only scores short tasks cannot tell you whether an agent stays on track over hours of operation.
