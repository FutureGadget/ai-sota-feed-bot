μ-Bench tests voice agents in shared interactions where several users must jointly complete one task. It has **800** simulated cases across **20** domains. Across **13** agents, proprietary systems reached about **62-65%** success, while open-weight models stayed below **20%**.

For builders: single-user voice benchmarks overstate readiness for shared settings like households, meetings, or support calls with several parties. If your agent serves more than one speaker, budget for a proprietary model or test open weights on this case directly.
