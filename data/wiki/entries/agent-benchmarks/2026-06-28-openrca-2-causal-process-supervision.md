title: "OpenRCA 2.0 grades root-cause analysis on the causal path, not just the answer"
date: 2026-06-28

---
OpenRCA 2.0 argues that labeling only the root cause reduces RCA to pattern matching. Its **PAVE** protocol uses known fault-injection interventions to reconstruct the causal propagation path from root cause to symptom, and grades agents step by step.

Grading the reasoning trajectory localizes a regression to the step that broke, instead of inferring it from a fallen aggregate score.
