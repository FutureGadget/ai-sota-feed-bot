**ExtractBench** evaluates schema-guided extraction: given a document and a user-defined schema, produce correct output with source evidence. It spans 4,869 pages across 370 enterprise documents, 8 business domains, and 67 document types, and is the first to score **value accuracy, record completeness, grounding, and measured cost** together.

A bare accuracy number hides whether an extraction can be traced to its source and what it cost.
