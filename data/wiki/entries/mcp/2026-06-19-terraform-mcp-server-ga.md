HashiCorp made its open-source **Terraform MCP Server** generally available. It lets agents work with Terraform Registry APIs, aimed at relieving infrastructure engineers of rote tasks.

An official infrastructure vendor now ships the server itself, so teams don't maintain their own wrapper around the registry.
