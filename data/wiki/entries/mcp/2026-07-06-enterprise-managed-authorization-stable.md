The MCP project promoted its **Enterprise-Managed Authorization** extension to stable. Organizations control access to MCP servers through their identity provider, replacing per-server consent prompts with a zero-touch flow: sign in once, then reach approved servers without further setup.

It turns what single vendors had shipped one-off into a spec any MCP client or server can implement.
