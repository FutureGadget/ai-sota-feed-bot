The **MCP 2026-07-28 specification** is the largest revision since launch: the protocol is **stateless by default**, gains a governed extensions system, and hardens authorization. Amazon Bedrock AgentCore Gateway already supports it; switching a gateway over is a single `UpdateGateway` call.

This rewrites how clients and servers interoperate rather than adding a connector. A managed gateway gives platform teams a reference for adoption without patching each client.
