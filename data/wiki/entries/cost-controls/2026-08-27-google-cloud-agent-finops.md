Google Cloud became the third major vendor to ship FinOps for agents, and goes further on flexibility:

- **Billing:** a pay-as-you-go Gemini Enterprise tier with no upfront commitment beside per-seat plans; Flexible Savings Plans give 10% (one year) or 20% (three years) off with no minimum commitment.
- **Anomaly detection** names a spike's root cause and its top three SKUs.
- **Project-level spend caps** pause API calls at a hard monthly limit, with alerts at 50/80/100%.
- **Deferred execution** (coming) runs eligible agent workloads in off-peak windows for up to 50% off inference.

Anomaly root-causing and off-peak scheduling are two levers the OpenAI and Anthropic consoles do not offer yet.
