Mole is an open-source deep-research agent for the terminal. Its author built it because research agents "blow way past budget, jumble the sources," and still sound confident. Mole **never spends more than its budget (measured overshoot is 0%)**, attaches a source to every claim, and keeps local data on the machine.

It shows runaway spend is still the default without an enforced ceiling, told from the tool builder's side. The fix is a cap the agent cannot exceed, not an alert after the run.
