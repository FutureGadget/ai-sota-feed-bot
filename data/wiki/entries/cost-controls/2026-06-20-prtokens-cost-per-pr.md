Prtokens, an open-source tool, attributes **agent token cost to an individual pull request**, so cost becomes a number on the artifact rather than an aggregate on the monthly bill.

Per-unit-of-work attribution is what lets a team ask whether a given change was worth what the agent spent on it, which is the starting point for any cost-per-task or ROI argument.
