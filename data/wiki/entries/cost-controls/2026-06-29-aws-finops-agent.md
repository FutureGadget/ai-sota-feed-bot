AWS released **FinOps Agent** in public preview. It investigates cost anomalies, correlates spend changes with AWS activity data, and routes findings to resource owners through Slack and Jira.

Anomaly triage becomes continuous and queryable instead of a manual monthly dig through the bill. Cost analysis is itself becoming an agentic product, though it still depends on the tagging and attribution a team has in place.
