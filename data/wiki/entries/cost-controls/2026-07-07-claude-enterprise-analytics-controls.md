Anthropic added richer admin analytics, **model-level entitlements**, and spend alerts to Claude Enterprise. Admins can track adoption, restrict which models each group can use, and get warned before spend crosses a line.

Entitlements are a cost control in their own right: limiting who can call the most expensive model bounds spend without a per-request cap. Teams get attribution and capping centrally instead of each one building its own metering.
