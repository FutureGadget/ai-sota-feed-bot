OpenAI shipped **usage analytics plus updated spend controls** for ChatGPT Enterprise, so an org can set ceilings on spend instead of discovering overruns on the invoice.

It moves cost from a monthly surprise to a signal admins watch and bound. For teams on a single vendor, this is the cheapest way to get a cap in place before building their own metering layer.
