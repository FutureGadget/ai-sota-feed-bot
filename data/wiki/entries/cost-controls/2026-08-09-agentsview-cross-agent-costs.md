Agentsview, an open-source tool, lets a developer browse, search, and **track costs across all of their AI coding agents** in one place.

Each vendor's dashboard shows only its own agent. A developer or team running several agents needs a cross-agent view to see total spend, and third-party tooling is filling that gap.
