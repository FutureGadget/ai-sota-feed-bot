NVIDIA's work with AWS across Amazon OpenSearch and Amazon EC2 is framed around four constraints: **low-latency inference**, fast vector search, GPU price-performance, and infrastructure that grows without multiplying operational complexity.

Infra vendors now sell explicitly on latency. For an agent, raw engine speed moves only one term of the budget; loop length and tool round-trips still multiply it.
