OpenAI describes building **GPT-Live**, a continuous voice system with a turnless speech model (no push-to-talk turn-taking) and a low-latency architecture, in six months.

It is a model provider's own engineering case study of hitting the interactive latency floor, from the product side rather than a serving vendor's benchmark.
