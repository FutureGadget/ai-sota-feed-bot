title: "For enterprise data integration, agentic RAG buys accuracy that naive RAG lacks, at a cost to measure"
date: 2026-07-27

---
A vision paper on LLM-driven data integration finds naive RAG keeps hitting **accuracy and cost limits** in enterprise settings because of a persistent knowledge gap. It argues for knowledge-grounded agents in an agentic RAG loop that retrieves, checks, and re-queries, with integration decisions backed by retrieved evidence.

The paper frames agentic RAG as a trade to measure against the naive baseline, not an automatic upgrade: each extra loop buys accuracy with more calls.
