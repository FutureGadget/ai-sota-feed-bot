LangChain built its internal data agent on **Hex, dbt, semantic models, and observability tooling**, and reports a **40x increase in self-service analysis**.

The governed semantic layer under the agent, not a better retrieval trick on top, is what made answers trustworthy. Grounding a data agent is a data-engineering investment. See [agent observability](/topic/agent-observability) for the tracing side.
