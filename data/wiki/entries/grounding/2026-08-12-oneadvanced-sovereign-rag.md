OneAdvanced, a UK enterprise software provider, built a **UK-sovereign AI platform** on AWS: Llama 4 Maverick and Llama Guard 4 self-hosted on SageMaker AI, a **RAG pipeline on pgvector**, and more than 50 agents built with the Strands Agents SDK on ECS.

Here data residency, not cost or latency, drove the self-hosted retrieval stack, and the model choice and retrieval architecture were decided together.
