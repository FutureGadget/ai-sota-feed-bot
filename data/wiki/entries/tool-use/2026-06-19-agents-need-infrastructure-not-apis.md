A messaging-infrastructure vendor argues that agents need **purpose-built, agent-native endpoints**, not SMS-style REST APIs designed for human developers and wrapped after the fact. Its path runs from plain REST APIs to an MCP surface.

It states the greenfield position: design the capability for an agent caller from the start. Most enterprises cannot rebuild their estate this way, which is why brownfield overlays appear alongside it.
