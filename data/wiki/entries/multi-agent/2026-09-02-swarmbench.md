SwarmBench evaluates models as orchestrators of **dynamic agent swarms**, scoring accuracy, efficiency, cost, and process quality rather than only the final answer. Current models **differ substantially in orchestration capability**. The authors also introduce SwarmExp, an experience-extraction-and-replay method they report improves orchestration consistently.

Orchestration is a model skill you can measure and select for, not a free byproduct of model size. See [agent benchmarks](/topic/agent-benchmarks).
