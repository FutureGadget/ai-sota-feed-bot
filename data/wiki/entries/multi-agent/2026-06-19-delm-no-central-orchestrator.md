Stanford's DeLM reports cutting multi-agent task cost by **roughly half by removing the central orchestrator**.

A single coordinating agent is both a token bottleneck and a single point of failure. Decentralized topologies are harder to trace and can deadlock or diverge, so the saving comes with an observability bill. See [agent cost](/topic/agent-cost).
