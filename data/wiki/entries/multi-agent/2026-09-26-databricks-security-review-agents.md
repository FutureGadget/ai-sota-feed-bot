Databricks' earlier security-review automation had plateaued at predictable requests. The replacement uses **seven specialized agents** (intake, risk assessment, requirements, browser-extension and vendor review, validation, workflow, learning) behind an intake app, a reasoning layer that **routes by task complexity to Haiku, Sonnet, or Opus**, serverless orchestration, and a governed data layer.

Eligible routine requests now clear in minutes instead of days, while novel or high-risk decisions go to people. The results are self-reported.
