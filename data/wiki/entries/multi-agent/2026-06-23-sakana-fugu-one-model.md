Sakana's **Fugu** collapses a multi-agent system into one model, trading the coordination layer away once the division of labor is known.

It is the far end of the "is it worth it" question: sometimes the cheapest topology is no topology at all. The cost is that you can no longer swap, inspect, or trace individual roles.
