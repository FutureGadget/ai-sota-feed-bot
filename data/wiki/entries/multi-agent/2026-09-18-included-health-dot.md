Included Health's **Dot** is a main LangGraph router agent plus domain-owned sub-workflows (urgent-care intake, scheduling, specialist finding, behavioral health) built on Deep Agents, so product teams own separate sub-workflows on shared platform capabilities.
- On uncertainty, the router **pauses, hands the conversation to a human care advocate, and resumes with full context**.
- Clinicians review every conversation for correct routing and emergency escalation.
- Reported: 75% lift in chat engagement, over 95% clinician agreement on care recommendations, over 99% detection of high-risk situations on audit.

Safety-critical domains need a continuous review loop, not just a one-shot approval gate.
