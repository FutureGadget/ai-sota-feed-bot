DPBench studies the **structural determinants of multi-agent LLM coordination**: which topologies and role assignments make collaboration pay off and which only add noise. The communication structure comes out as the dominant variable.

Before adding agents, decide who talks to whom and in what format. That choice moves results more than the number of agents.
