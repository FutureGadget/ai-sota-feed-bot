AWS argues multi-agent systems **fail in ways traditional monitoring misses** and pairs two layers: **Amazon Bedrock AgentCore Evaluations** for continuous quality scoring and **AWS DevOps Agent** for autonomous infrastructure investigation, shown on a four-agent airline reservation system.

Quality regressions and infrastructure faults look different and need different watchers. See [agent tracing](/topic/agent-tracing) for the capture layer both depend on.
