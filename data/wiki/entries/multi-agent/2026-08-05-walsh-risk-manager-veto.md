title: "Walsh gives a risk-manager agent veto power over trades proposed by analysis agents"
date: 2026-08-05
theme: oversight-and-safety
evidence: [b1f71fce6d0aa52b]
---
Walsh is a multi-agent research pipeline for trading in which independent analysis agents do the research and a **dedicated risk-manager agent can veto a trade outright**, not just score or rank it.

It is a harder-edged arbiter: the overseeing agent's job is to stop an action before it happens, not to judge it afterward.
