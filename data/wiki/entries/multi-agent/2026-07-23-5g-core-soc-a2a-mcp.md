A multi-agent architecture built on **A2A and MCP** in a live 5G-core security operations center targets the real bottleneck: keeping the detection rule base aligned with threats that evolve faster than rules get written, not analyst triage. It reports **mean time to detect and respond down 40%** and the human work required **compressed 12x**.

Coordination overhead can pay for itself in production when the task genuinely decomposes. The figures are the author's own.
