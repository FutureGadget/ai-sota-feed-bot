Anthropic's model-selection guide tells buyers to compare model classes on **cost per task, not cost per token**, then settle the choice with evals built for their own workload rather than a leaderboard score.

It folds model selection into the ROI calculation. A cheaper per-token model that needs more tokens or retries can cost more per task, and only a workload-specific eval shows which way it goes.
