Sarah Friar, OpenAI's CFO, proposed a practical AI scorecard built on four measures: **useful work delivered, cost per successful task, dependability, and return on compute**.

It is the per-task attribution engineers argue for, pushed by a finance function. That suggests cost per successful task is becoming the shared ROI vocabulary, not one platform team's convention. It also arrives from a vendor that benefits from compute spend, so treat it as a framing, not a neutral standard.
