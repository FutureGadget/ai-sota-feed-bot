The **Claude apps gateway for AWS** is a self-hosted control plane that gives an org one point of control over **access, cost, and policy** for Claude Code and Claude Desktop, running on Amazon Bedrock and Claude Platform on AWS.

It moves governance past a vendor's own admin console into infrastructure the org runs itself. The trade is the usual one: more control over attribution and policy, plus a service to operate.
