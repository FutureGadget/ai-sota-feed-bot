raidho, a Show HN coding agent, replaces RAG-style nearest-neighbour lookup with **vector-symbolic architecture (VSA)** memory: facts are combined with algebraic binding and bundling operations rather than retrieved by embedding distance.

It is an early, single-project signal. It belongs to a wider argument that exact, structured recall can beat fuzzy similarity for some agent workloads.
