Target retrieves similar historical marketing campaigns for forecasting with **embeddings plus vector search plus LLM ranking**, replacing a rule-based workflow. It reports **75% top-1 and 100% top-3 coverage**, and feeds campaign outcomes back to refine retrieval.

A production example where the gain comes from how records are embedded and reranked, and from a feedback loop, not from the choice of vector store.
