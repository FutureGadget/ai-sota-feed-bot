Google's AlloyDB packages **vector and hybrid search, natural-language-to-SQL, and AI functions** that call foundation models such as Gemini directly on database rows, with new performance and cost improvements to those functions.

A general-purpose operational database now doubles as the retrieval layer, so structured filters and similarity search run in one query against the system of record.
