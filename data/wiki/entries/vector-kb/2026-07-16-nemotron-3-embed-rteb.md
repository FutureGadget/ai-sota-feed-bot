NVIDIA's Nemotron 3 Embed line ranks **#1 overall on RTEB**, a multilingual, multi-domain retrieval benchmark, at 78.5% for the flagship 8B model. The smaller **1B variant cuts its predecessor's error rate by 27%**.

Swapping the embedder raises the ceiling of every retrieval architecture built on it without touching the index, chunking, or rerank. Better retrieval also means an agent finds evidence in fewer search calls, so it is a cost and latency lever as well as an accuracy one.
