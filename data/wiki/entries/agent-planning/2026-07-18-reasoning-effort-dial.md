Sebastian Raschka explains how LLMs learn **low, medium, and high reasoning-effort modes**, which providers now expose to trade latency and cost for deliberation depth.

A harness can set "how hard should the model think before acting here" per step, instead of applying one fixed reasoning budget to every step of a plan.
