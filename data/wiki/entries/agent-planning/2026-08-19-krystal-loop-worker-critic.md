The **Krystal Loop Protocol** structures a coding agent's loop as a **bounded worker/critic pair**: one role proposes work, a second checks it, within an explicit bound rather than an open-ended retry.

It packages the propose-then-check structure as a named, reusable protocol instead of a bespoke harness detail.
