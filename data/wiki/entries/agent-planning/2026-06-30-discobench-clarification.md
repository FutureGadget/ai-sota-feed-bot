Most search-agent benchmarks assume the user's query is complete. **DiscoBench** measures clarification-aware deep search: whether an agent recognizes an **under-specified goal and asks** instead of confidently planning down the wrong path.

It treats "ask a question" as a first-class planning action that a harness should test for, not a sign of weakness.
