Introspection co-founder Roland Gavrilescu describes **autoresearch**: agents iterate on their own task **recipes** across runs, closing a feedback loop over the plan itself rather than over individual steps.

He is explicit that humans remain central to steering the loop. Self-improving plans still need someone choosing what "better" means.
