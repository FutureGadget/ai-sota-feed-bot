Paul Bakaus, discussing his tool Impeccable, argues against **one-shot AI design**: iterative, human-curated task specs ("skills") get better plans than hoping a single prompt gets them right, even in a "loopmaxxing" era.

Treat task specs as artifacts you refine across runs, with human judgment in the loop.
