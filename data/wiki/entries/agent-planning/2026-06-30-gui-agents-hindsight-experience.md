title: "GUI agents that explore and reuse hindsight experience plan repetitive tasks better"
date: 2026-06-30

---
Small open multimodal web agents are cheap and private but plan poorly and generalize weakly across websites. This work has them **autonomously explore** interfaces and reuse **hindsight experience** from earlier attempts, which improves task decomposition on repetitive GUI tasks over zero-shot planning.

A plan learned from prior runs beats one re-derived cold each time, which matters most when you want a small model instead of a frontier one.
