---
title: "Research agents recover better by weighing several failure hypotheses than by one reflection"
date: 2026-07-04
theme: verify-and-replan
evidence: [5a5b80258f0f8836]
---
Autonomous research agents usually recover from a failed experiment with a **single free-form reflection** that compresses metrics, logs, and design choices into one critique. This work generates **several candidate explanations** for the failure, weighs them, and re-plans around the most likely cause.

Failure diagnosis becomes a planning step, not a trigger for blind retry.
