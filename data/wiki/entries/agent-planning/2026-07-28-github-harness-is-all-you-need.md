GitHub's "The harness is all you need (mostly)" lays out a repeatable Copilot workflow (**prototype, plan, implement, review**) and argues for building discipline around it instead of re-architecting every time a new AI tool ships.

A second major coding-agent vendor backs the loop-as-infrastructure view: harness stability is worth more than chasing novelty.
