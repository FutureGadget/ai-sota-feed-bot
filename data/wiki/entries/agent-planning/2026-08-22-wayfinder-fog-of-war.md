Matt Pocock's **/wayfinder** skill is for greenfield projects or work where the way forward is unclear. It treats planning as navigating a "**fog of war**": explore first, then commit to a plan.

It adds a third option beside asking for clarification and proceeding: scout the territory when decomposition is not obvious up front.
