**Agent RFT** fine-tunes reasoning models with reinforcement learning against **real-time tool interactions and custom reward signals**. The InfoQ talk frames it as solving credit assignment: which of many steps in a long trajectory caused success or failure.

Speakers report enterprise cases where it removed **long-tail token loops**. Training now targets planning directly, not only the harness around it.
