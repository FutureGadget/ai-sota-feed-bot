Netflix open-sourced an agentic workflow for **observational causal inference**. Given observational data and the analyst's own plan, an **actor-critic loop** estimates causality, writes a report, and suggests next steps, reducing analysis toil.

The worker/critic structure from coding agents carries over to a data-science task, with the human supplying the plan the agent executes against.
