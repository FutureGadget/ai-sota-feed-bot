Lilian Weng's survey names three recurring harness patterns: goal-oriented **plan, execute, observe, improve** loops; the **file system as persistent memory**; and parent agents spawning inspectable sub-agents. It goes further by treating the **harness code itself** as an artifact an LLM mutation operator can evolve (AlphaEvolve, Darwin Gödel Machine).

Her caveat matters as much as the taxonomy: self-improvement loops are only as good as their evaluation signal, and weak evaluators remain the bottleneck. Pair any harness-evolution experiment with [trajectory-level eval](/topic/agent-evaluation).
