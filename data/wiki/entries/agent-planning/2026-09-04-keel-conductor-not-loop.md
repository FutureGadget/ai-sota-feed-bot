**Keel** (release 0.10) bills itself as "a conductor, not an agent loop" for AI coding agents that **prove their work**.

It is an explicit alternative to the loop-as-primitive framing most harnesses assume: an orchestrator that directs agents and checks evidence, rather than one open loop. The Show HN post gives no measured results.
