"Agents in the Wild" surveys agentic systems moving from research prototypes to production in software engineering, scientific discovery, and finance. It contrasts academic benchmark focus with deployment needs.

The deployment evidence backs the structure-around-the-loop thesis: decomposition, checkpoints, and guardrails carry production systems, not a stronger model alone.
