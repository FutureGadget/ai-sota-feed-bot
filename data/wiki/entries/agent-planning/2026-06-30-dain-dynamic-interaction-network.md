DAIN (Dynamic Agent-based Interaction Network) reframes multimodal fusion as a **dynamic network of interacting agents** whose collaboration and reasoning structure adapts per task, instead of a static mixture-of-experts routing.

The planning lesson: the structure of who reasons about what can itself be chosen per task rather than fixed up front.
