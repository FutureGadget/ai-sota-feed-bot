**PoTRE** (Poly-Topological Reasoning Ensembles) runs four heterogeneous agents (adversarial refinement, hierarchical strategic planning, spectrum search, direct chain) and reconciles them through a task-adaptive aggregation layer using candidate selection, semantic synthesis, or neuro-symbolic verification.

It reaches **49.92% on Humanity's Last Exam**, above the previous best official score, with similar or fewer inference tokens than scaled single-stream baselines. Decomposing long-horizon reasoning across specialized roles can beat scaling one reasoner at comparable cost; see [multi-agent](/topic/multi-agent).
