Most multi-hop benchmarks are short-horizon, needing only a few retrieval or inference steps. **Tasks over Application Manuals (TAM)** tests whether a model can follow real procedures spanning **hundreds of pages of interdependent guidelines**, and finds gaps that short benchmarks miss.

It tests whether a plan stays consistent with a long manual, not a small task graph; related long-horizon gaps are tracked on [agent evaluation](/topic/agent-evaluation).
