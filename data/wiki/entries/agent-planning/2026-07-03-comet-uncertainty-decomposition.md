CoMet targets uncertainty estimation in multimodal models by decomposing **what kind of uncertainty** a model has, from context and from multiplicity of valid answers.

"Knowing what you don't know" is the input a planning loop needs to choose between asking a clarifying question and proceeding. CoMet works on producing that signal; benchmarks like DiscoBench only score the decision.
