Stanford's DeLM reports cutting multi-agent task cost by **about 50% by removing the central orchestrator** while keeping target completion rates.

The coordination structure, not only the model, dominates multi-agent spend. Topology is a cost decision to make before choosing models; see [orchestration](/topic/agent-orchestration).
