DeepSeek released another cheaper model as China's domestic **price war intensifies** (Semafor). A week later, DeepSeek V4 Flash was reported running a **full test suite for $72, about 33x cheaper than Kimi K3** on the same job.

The price war is not only cutting list prices; it is widening the gap between individual open-weight releases. Cost per job varies by more than an order of magnitude among models of similar class, so benchmark cost on your own tasks.
