GitHub's **Project HydraFusion**, a research preview in Copilot, routes each coding step to the model that step needs. In **controlled offline evaluations** its selective workflows matched or exceeded an Opus 5 baseline while reducing estimated workflow cost.

It is selective multi-model orchestration shipped inside a mainstream coding assistant rather than a benchmark paper. The results are offline and the savings are estimates, so production numbers are still to come.
