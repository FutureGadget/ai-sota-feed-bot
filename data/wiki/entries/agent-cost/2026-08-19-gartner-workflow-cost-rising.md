Gartner forecasts that **inference cost per agentic workflow will increase more than fivefold through 2028**, even as per-token prices fall.

Workflow spend scales with the steps and tool calls an agent takes, not with the price of any one token. A falling price list does not guarantee a falling bill once workflows get more agentic.
