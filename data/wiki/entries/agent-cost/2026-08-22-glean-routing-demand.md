Glean CEO Arvind Jain argues that **frontier model cost and the popularity of open-weight models** are driving organizations toward model routing to control AI spend. Glean improves its own routing with **human feedback loops at scale**.

It is the buyer-side case for routing: a router whose decisions improve from production feedback, rather than a fixed table, is what lets cheaper models take traffic safely.
