Anthropic's guide to steering Claude Code compares seven methods (skills, hooks, subagents, rules and more) by **the context cost and the authority of each**.

Steering and cost are the same knob seen from two sides. Each instruction added to shape behavior consumes context budget on every turn, so a team should pick the cheapest mechanism that carries enough authority for the rule.
