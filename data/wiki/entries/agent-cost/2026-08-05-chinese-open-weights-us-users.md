Reporting in Memeburn finds **Chinese AI models gaining US users** on the strength of lower costs and open weights.

It is an early signal of the shift later measured in gateway traffic: price, not only capability, is moving where agent workloads run. Open weights also give buyers a self-hosting option that caps their exposure to any one vendor's price changes.
