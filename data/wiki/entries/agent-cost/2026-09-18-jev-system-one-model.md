TypeSafe's **Jev** is pitched as a "System One Model" that only decides, classifies, routes, or scores. It reports running **more than 100x faster and more than 200x cheaper** than small frontier LLMs on those narrow jobs.

It packages the "most turns don't need frontier capability" finding as a purpose-built model rather than a policy over general models. The figures are the vendor's own; the router or classifier step in a harness is where to test it.
