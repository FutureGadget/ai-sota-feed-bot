In an OpenAI case study, Parallel's research agents cut **both research time and API cost in half** on the same labor-market synthesis task after moving to GPT-6 Astra, versus the prior model generation.

A newer model that finishes in fewer, cheaper steps can lower cost per task even when it is not the cheapest per token. It is a launch-day, vendor-published single customer result.
