OpenAI shipped **GPT-6 Sol and Luna**, two GPT-6-family models pitched explicitly as **different balances of capability and cost** for everyday work, rather than a single frontier tier.

Vendors now ship the tier ladder that routers pick from. A team can route between models in one family, with shared behavior and tooling, instead of mixing providers to get a cheap tier.
