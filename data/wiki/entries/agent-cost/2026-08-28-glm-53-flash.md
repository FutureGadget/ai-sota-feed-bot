Zhipu's **GLM-5.3-Flash** scores within three points of its larger GLM-5.3 on Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index at **roughly a seventh of the cost**, with all inference running on Chinese chips rather than Nvidia hardware.

A small quality gap at a large price gap is the shape that makes Flash tiers the default for routed bulk traffic. Open SWE later used it as its cheapest routing tier.
