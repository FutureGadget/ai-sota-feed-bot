Open-weight models passed proprietary ones by token volume on Vercel's AI Gateway for the first time: **54% on one day and a record 62% on another, up from 28% on June 24**. DeepSeek-V4-Flash was the single most-used model, with Chinese models (StepFun's Step 3.7 Flash, Zhipu's GLM-5.2) filling out the top five.

Price pressure now shows up in where traffic goes, not only in list prices. One gateway's mix is not the market, but it is measured routing behavior rather than a forecast.
