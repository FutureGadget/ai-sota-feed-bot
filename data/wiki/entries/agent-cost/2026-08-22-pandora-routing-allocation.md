title: "Model routing framed as allocation when estimating each answer's value is costly"
date: 2026-08-22

"Pandora's AI Model Routing Box" treats routing across heterogeneous models, harnesses, and inference settings as **efficient allocation under costly value estimation**: to route well you must estimate which specialist answers best at the lowest cost, and that estimate itself costs compute.

It gives routing a formal footing instead of a heuristic, and names the hidden cost a router adds: the price of deciding.
