Millwright is an open-source, **Rust-based, self-hosted LLM router** built for cost savings, transparency, and performance. Its author launched it as hosted routers proliferate (Ramp Router, Vercel's AI Gateway) and as OpenRouter faces a possible acquisition.

Owning the router gives a team control over per-request model choice without depending on a vendor's continuity. The trade is operational: provider integrations, updates, and uptime become the team's job.
