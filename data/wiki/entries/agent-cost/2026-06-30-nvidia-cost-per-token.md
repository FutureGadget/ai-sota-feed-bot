NVIDIA frames infrastructure buying around **tokens per dollar and per watt** rather than peak chip specs. Its numbers:

- GB300 NVL72 delivers **10-25x the performance per watt** of Hopper across three current open models.
- A further **5x software-only gain** on one model within a month, from quantization, disaggregated serving, and KV-cache offloading.
- Power-shifting software runs **up to 40% more GPUs** in the same power budget.

These are vendor figures. For self-hosted agents, the serving stack sets the floor price every other optimization multiplies against.
