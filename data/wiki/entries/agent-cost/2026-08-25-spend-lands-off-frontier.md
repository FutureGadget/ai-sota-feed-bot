An FT story on the Ramp AI Index (credit-card billing data from about 70,000 companies) shows **Opus 5, then weeks old, capturing 3.5% of Anthropic spend in July**, while the prior Opus 4.8 took 28%. Anthropic's annualized revenue still rose to **$65bn from $47bn in May**.

The spend is real and growing; it just does not concentrate on the newest frontier model. That supports the routing and effort arguments: most per-task spend does not need frontier capability.
