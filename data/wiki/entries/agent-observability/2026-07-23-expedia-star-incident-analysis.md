Expedia's **STAR**, built on FastAPI, Datadog, Celery, Redis, and Langfuse, ingests service telemetry during incidents, runs **structured workflows** to generate root-cause assessments, and keeps engineers in the loop for the final call.

It is the trace-plus-LLM-analysis pattern built on infrastructure a platform team already runs, not a new observability product.
