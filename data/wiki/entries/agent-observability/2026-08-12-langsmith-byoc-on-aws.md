LangSmith's **Bring Your Own Cloud** option is generally available on AWS, giving enterprise teams managed observability, evaluation, and deployment **inside their own VPC**.

It meets the "keep traces in our network" requirement from the vendor side: the Claude Apps Gateway answers it by self-hosting, BYOC by deploying the managed product into the customer's cloud.
