A Coroot experiment running root-cause analysis across **eleven models** offers early evidence that modern LLMs can reason through RCA once given correctly prepared context.

The hard problem moves from model reasoning to the pipelines that correlate telemetry into that context, the work Expedia's STAR invests in.
