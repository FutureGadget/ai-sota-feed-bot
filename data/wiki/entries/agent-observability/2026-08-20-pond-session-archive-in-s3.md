**Pond**, a Show HN project, stores agent sessions from multiple machines in **one S3 bucket you own**, with no database service to run. Sessions are searchable and exposed to the agent as an MCP server.

Session history becomes a durable, portable record under the team's control rather than local files tied to one laptop or tool.
