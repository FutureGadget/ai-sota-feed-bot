Google Cloud's lessons on accelerating foundation-model upgrades start from the observation that moving to a new model or checkpoint (for example, to Gemini 3.5) usually means **months of manual testing** to prove quality and evaluate new responses.

The migration path, not the deprecation notice, is where drift has to be managed. See [version pinning](/topic/version-pinning).
