Vercel's November AI Gateway index, as summarized by a secondary write-up, puts a production model's half-life at about six weeks.

- **Churn:** a model peaks within a month of launch, and four weeks later three in four of the teams using it at peak have moved on.
- **Recency:** more than half of October's language-model requests went to models under 60 days old. The median model family ships a new version every 61 days.
- **Switching:** about half of teams above 10 million tokens a month changed their most-used model in October, and six in ten of those switches crossed labs.

Model swaps are a roughly monthly event, so regression suites and prompt portability get exercised on that cadence. Vendor-reported from one gateway's traffic.
