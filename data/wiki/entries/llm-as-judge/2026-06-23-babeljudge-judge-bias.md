BabelJudge quantifies LLM-as-judge reliability across languages and agent trajectories. It finds **position bias** (favoring slot A), **verbosity bias**, and language-dependent drift that raw accuracy masks.

A judge is only as trustworthy as the bias and agreement numbers you can show against held-out human labels.
