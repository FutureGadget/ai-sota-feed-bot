Dan Luu's notes on agentic coding report that running **independent judges under different personas** — including one briefed to argue the opposite verdict — over the same artifact cuts false positives more reliably than upgrading to a stronger single model.

The cost is more judge calls per artifact, so it pays off where false positives are expensive to triage.
