LangSmith's **Tuned Evaluators** attach quality feedback directly to production traces, starting with a **Perceived Error** signal, so teams can find and fix agent mistakes from what users actually experienced.

It complements trace mining for failure clusters: the grading signal starts from user-perceived failure rather than from a rubric someone wrote up front.
