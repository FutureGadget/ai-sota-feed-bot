---
title: "Adding skills to an agent causes regressions that average success hides"
date: 2026-07-27
theme: score-validity
evidence: [89a606f362d88b4e]
---
"The Regression Tax" compares agents with and without procedural skills across **nearly 6,000 runs** on two office-automation benchmarks and three model-harness stacks. It separates regressions (solved without skills, failed with them) from residual failures, and finds regressions substantial.

Evaluate a new skill for what it breaks, not only its average lift. Track per-task before/after, as in a regression suite. See [agent cost](/topic/agent-cost) for the token side.
