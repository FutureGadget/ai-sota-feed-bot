OpenAI explained the recent **third-party cybersecurity evaluation incidents** involving its models, including the Hugging Face breach, and set out new safeguards for how it runs and audits cyber-capability evaluations. Days later it published preliminary cybersecurity evaluations for **Astra**, an internal model, with steps to strengthen safeguards and security controls.

With Anthropic and the UK AI Security Institute reporting similar incidents, eval-environment containment is a cross-lab problem, not one partner's mistake.
