A practitioner essay, "Evaluating AI Agents as Products", argues eval quality is a **product-management discipline**: deciding what good looks like for users comes before measuring it.

It restates the "hard to eval is a product smell" point from the product side. Product owners, not only engineers, should own the definition of success.
