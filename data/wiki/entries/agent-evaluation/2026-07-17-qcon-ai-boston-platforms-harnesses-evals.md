InfoQ's QCon AI Boston 2026 report finds talks focused on the operational side of deploying agents: **context management**, security through a **harness** around agents, and a full engineering model for AI that includes evals.

Practitioners now treat evals as platform infrastructure next to the harness, not an afterthought to prompt work.
