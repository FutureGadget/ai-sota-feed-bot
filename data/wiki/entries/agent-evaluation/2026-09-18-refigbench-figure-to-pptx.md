**ReFigBench** benchmarks multimodal coding agents on reconstructing scientific figures as **editable PowerPoint artifacts**. It notes that proxies like short tool calls or screenshot resemblance cannot tell whether a low score came from poor perception, poor planning, or the harness.

It evaluates the usable artifact, and treats the harness as part of the system under test.
