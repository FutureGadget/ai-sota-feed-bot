**BenchMIRT** applies multidimensional item response theory to individual questions across 16 benchmarks and 100 models. Two latent dimensions dominate — safety and general reasoning — and some benchmarks land on the "wrong" one: BBQ (built for bias) tracks general reasoning, and WMDP correlates negatively with reasoning because refusal is the desired answer.

Keeping **10% of a benchmark's questions** preserves nearly the same capability picture. Benchmark size and measurement value are not the same thing, and a smaller suite can be cheaper to run per release.
