The AI Evaluator Forum's **AEF-1** baseline standard sets criteria for third-party evaluators' access, conflicts of interest, funding relationships, recusal, and transparency; **xAI, OpenAI, and Anthropic** cosigned it.

Anthropic's commitment is the most concrete: embedded evaluators such as METR get desks, badges, and laptops, with permissions comparable to an internal risk-assessment team, assessing shipped models and training pipelines continuously rather than auditing after the fact.
