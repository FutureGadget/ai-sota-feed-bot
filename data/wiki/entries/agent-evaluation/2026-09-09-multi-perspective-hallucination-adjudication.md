A medical hallucination-detection study argues factual-error detection is wrongly treated as a **single-pass, single-annotator** labeling problem. In long-form chatbot responses, errors are subtle and embedded in mostly-correct text, so it develops a multi-perspective annotation protocol that goes beyond majority vote.

The ground truth for a hallucination detector needs auditing too; one grader's call is not a reliable label.
