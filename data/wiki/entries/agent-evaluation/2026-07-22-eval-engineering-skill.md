LangChain's **Eval Engineering Skill** inspects an agent's repo and production traces, proposes evals through **user interviews** rather than a blank rubric, and outputs runnable Harbor tasks.

Writing evals is the cost that stalls most teams. Making eval authorship an agent job, grounded in real traces, attacks that cost directly.
