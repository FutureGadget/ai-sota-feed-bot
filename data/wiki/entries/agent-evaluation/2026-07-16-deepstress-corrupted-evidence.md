title: "DeepStress shows search agents vary widely when fed unreliable evidence"
date: 2026-07-16
theme: domain-benchmarks
evidence: [9f3ebb1dd514f218]
---
**DeepStress** stress-tests deep search agents by controlling how often they meet challenging evidence — corrupted on trustworthiness, relevance, or factuality — instead of the clean documents standard benchmarks assume. Agents vary widely in how they handle it.

Poor evidence is rare in benchmark data but can cause dramatic failure in real use. If your agent searches the open web, test it on bad inputs on purpose.
