theme: gaming-and-containment

---
**Ponytail**, a single-author repo of coding-agent instruction files, passed 44,000 GitHub stars in nine days on a claim of **80-94% less code**. A contributor showed the baseline was flawed; the maintainer rebuilt the benchmark as a real agentic run and republished **54%**.

A benchmark number can mislead without anyone gaming it on purpose. Ask how the baseline was run before adopting a skill on its headline figure.
