---
title: "\"It's hard to eval\" is a product smell, not an excuse"
date: 2026-06-30
theme: eval-in-production
evidence: [979d921c237f1c0b]
---
Hamel Husain argues the common objection "our product is hard to eval" is a **product smell**: an artifact that is hard for you to verify is usually hard for users too, and in the worst case they redo the work to check it.

If you cannot say what good output looks like, fix the spec. Designing for ease of verification improves the product and makes the eval possible.
