OpenAI's **Deployment Simulation** runs a candidate model against real conversation data to forecast how it will behave once deployed, instead of measuring problems after an incident.

It pushes evaluation to the pre-release gate: the question becomes "what will this model do on our traffic" rather than "how did it score on a static benchmark".
