Hamel Husain and Shreya Shankar's **AI Evals FAQ**, distilled from teaching 5,000+ engineers and PMs, argues teams should spend **60-80% of eval development time on manual error analysis** — reading at least 100 diverse traces before automating anything.

It also warns that a **100% pass rate** means the suite is not challenging the system, not that the agent is done.
