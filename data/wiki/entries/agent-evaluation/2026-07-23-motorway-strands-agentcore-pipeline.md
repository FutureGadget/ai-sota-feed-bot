Motorway and AWS built an end-to-end evaluation pipeline on the **Strands Agents SDK** and **Amazon Bedrock AgentCore** that cut incorrect results from **1 in 8 queries to 1 in 50** and issue-detection time from hours to minutes.

It is a concrete before/after on what an eval pipeline is worth in production. The numbers come from a vendor blog.
