**SkillCorpus** filters roughly **821,000 crawled agent skills** (SKILL.md packages) into a curated, taxonomy-tagged corpus. Integrating it improves scores across three benchmarks and two harnesses.

The gains trace to a **coverage boundary** and a **harness boundary**: a good skill helps only the tasks it covers and only in a harness that can use it. Evaluate skills against your own task mix and harness.
