A production write-up, "Trust, but benchmark", describes letting an AI agent **optimize a live Elasticsearch deployment**, with every proposed change checked by benchmark before it was trusted.

Give an agent an optimization task only where a benchmark can verify each change. This is the AlphaEvolve constraint applied by a team on its own infrastructure.
