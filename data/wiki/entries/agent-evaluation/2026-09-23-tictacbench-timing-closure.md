**TicTacBench** evaluates coding agents on **timing closure** in register-transfer-level chip design. Existing RTL benchmarks score functional correctness and power/performance/area but leave timing closure under-evaluated.

Evaluation keeps fragmenting into narrow suites for individual engineering subskills rather than converging on one general agent benchmark.
