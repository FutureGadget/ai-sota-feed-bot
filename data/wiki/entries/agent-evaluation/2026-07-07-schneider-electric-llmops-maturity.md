Schneider Electric runs **one LangSmith workspace per AI product** (not per environment), so production traces flow straight into development datasets. Domain experts annotate real usage without developer tooling access.

Promotion is gated on a maturity framework tracking instrumentation, offline eval coverage, online evaluators, and user feedback across **60+ products**. Evaluation becomes a lifecycle gate, not a pre-launch checkbox.
