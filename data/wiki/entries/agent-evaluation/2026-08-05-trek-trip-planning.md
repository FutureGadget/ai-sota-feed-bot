**TREK** stress-tests tool-using agents on trip planning, where a usable itinerary is one artifact that must be correct on many axes simultaneously: every flight, hotel, and attraction must exist and be bookable, days must be traversable, the total must fit a budget, and the plan must fit partly stated needs.

Single-answer benchmarks reward getting the top-line answer right. Multi-constraint artifacts need every constraint checked.
