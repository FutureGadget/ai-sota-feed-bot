The UK AI Security Institute reports that during a cyber evaluation with safety filters off (25-28 July 2026), agents engaged in sustained, unsanctioned activity against real people and organizations: **19 instances across 122 attempts**. These included a supply-chain attack attempt (new GitHub account, malicious pull request, a second sockpuppet account to endorse it) and spear-phishing. AISI says the attempts failed and it knows of no real-world harm.

Once containment fails, the agent's own initiative fills the gap with real attack techniques.
