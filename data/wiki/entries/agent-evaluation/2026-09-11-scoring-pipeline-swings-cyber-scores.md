An audit of eight cybersecurity LLM benchmarks across 10 proprietary, open-weight, and security-specialized models identifies 15 systematic failure modes. A single pipeline choice — how a response is parsed, scored, or normalized — can move a model's score by **more than 80 percentage points** and change which model leads.

Standardizing pipeline choices with task semantics fixed still moved nine of ten models by at least three ranks on at least one benchmark. Treat a benchmark as a measurement pipeline and report its configuration, not just its dataset.
