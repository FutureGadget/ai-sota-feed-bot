---
title: "Agent benchmark scores can reflect protocol fit, not capability"
date: 2026-07-27
theme: score-validity
evidence: [ddce7e0a20f47f4f]
---
A protocol-validity analysis asks whether agent benchmarks measure capability at all. It argues many **conflate task difficulty with protocol and scaffolding artifacts**, so a score gain can reflect a better-fitted harness rather than a more capable agent.

When comparing harnesses or vendors, hold the protocol fixed or treat the harness as part of what is being measured.
