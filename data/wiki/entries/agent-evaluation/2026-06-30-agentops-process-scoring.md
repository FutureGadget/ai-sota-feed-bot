A multi-dataset benchmark for LLM agents doing microservice failure diagnosis (AgentOps) argues existing benchmarks are **outcome-oriented** — they score the final answer and skip the reasoning process. It adds two large-scale datasets over multimodal observability data and grades the diagnosis path.

Eval and [observability](/topic/agent-observability) converge here: the trace is the shared substrate for both debugging and grading.
