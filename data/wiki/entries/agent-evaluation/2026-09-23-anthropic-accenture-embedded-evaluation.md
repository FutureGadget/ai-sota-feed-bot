Anthropic is partnering with **Accenture** on independent evaluation of frontier AI, part of its commitment to embed evaluators inside the company. The two companies expect to invest **at least $1 billion** to build this capacity over the next five years.

It adds a second named outside partner to the embedded-evaluator model alongside METR, and puts real money behind third-party evaluation as an industry function.
