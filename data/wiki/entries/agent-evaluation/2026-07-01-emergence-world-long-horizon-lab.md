Emergence AI introduced **Emergence World**, a laboratory environment for evaluating **long-horizon agent autonomy** rather than single bounded tasks.

Long-running agents fail in ways a short task never exercises — drift, accumulated errors, lost goals — so they need environments that run long enough to show them.
