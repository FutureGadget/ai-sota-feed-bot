**Benchmark Radar** is a living database and search engine for AI benchmarks — LLM evaluation, agentic and tool-use, coding, reasoning, safety, and domain-specific suites — that helps locate datasets, code, and the settings behind reported scores.

With evaluation fragmenting into narrow suites, finding the right benchmark is itself a tooling problem.
