---
title: "OpenRCA 2.0 labels the failure propagation path, not just the root cause"
date: 2026-06-28
theme: grading-trajectories
evidence: [8957450e5744d59e]
---
OpenRCA 2.0 reframes root-cause-analysis evaluation from outcome labels to **causal process supervision**. Earlier RCA datasets labeled only the root cause, which reduced the task to pattern matching; the new labels include the propagation path from root cause to observed symptom.

That gives trajectory-aware graders something to be trained and audited against: did the agent reason through the right intermediate steps, not only land the final answer.
