LangSmith traces voice agents built on **Pipecat, LiveKit, OpenAI Realtime, and Gemini Live**, capturing audio, STT and TTS latency, interruptions, and tool calls in one trace.

The same span discipline now covers a real-time, turn-taking interface. For voice agents, per-stage latency and interruption events are the debugging data a text trace never had.
