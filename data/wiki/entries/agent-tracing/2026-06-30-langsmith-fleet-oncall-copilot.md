LangSmith's June 2026 release adds a **Fleet on-call copilot for alert triage**, voice trace debugging, and experiment status tracking.

Trace reading becomes an assistive workflow: the copilot starts from the alert and the traces behind it instead of an engineer scrolling spans. Like any model-over-trace analyzer it can be wrong or miss a rare failure, so treat its triage as a lead, not a verdict.
