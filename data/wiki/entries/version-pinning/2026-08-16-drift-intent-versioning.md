**Drift** is an open-source, **intent-driven versioning** tool for AI coding agents, launched on Hacker News.

It packages the discipline this page describes, tracking what an agent is meant to do across releases, as a dedicated tool instead of something each team assembles from lockfiles and CI gates. It is an early project with no published adoption data.
