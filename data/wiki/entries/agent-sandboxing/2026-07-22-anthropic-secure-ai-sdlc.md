Anthropic's Deputy CISO Jason Clinton describes how its security engineering team secures a development lifecycle in which **AI authors roughly 80% of merged code**. The account stacks scoped access, monitoring, and review controls across the whole pipeline instead of relying on one sandbox or guardrail.

It is a practitioner case for defense in depth with no single trusted layer, at the scale of an entire engineering org.
