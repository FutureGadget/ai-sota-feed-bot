OneCLI (YC S26) is an open-source agent harness for teams. Each employee gets a **sandboxed personal agent** with connectors (GitHub, Gmail, Notion, Dropbox) attached from chat, **deterministic in-chat approval** for irreversible actions such as sending an email or deleting a ticket, and one team policy enforced across every agent in the workspace.

The same controls as per-developer harness settings, administered centrally by a platform team.
