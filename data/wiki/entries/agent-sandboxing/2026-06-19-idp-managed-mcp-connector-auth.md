Claude now lets admins provision MCP connectors for the whole organization **through their identity provider, starting with Okta**. Users get connector access on first login, with authorization configured centrally.

Tool access becomes explicit, centrally granted, and revocable through the IdP, instead of ambient per-user OAuth grants scattered across laptops. See [MCP](/topic/mcp).
