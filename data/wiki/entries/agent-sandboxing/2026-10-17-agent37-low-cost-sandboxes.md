Agent37 (YC F26) launched on Hacker News with sandboxes for running AI agents. The founder says customers average **$0.37/month per sandbox**, at under 10% of the cost of most sandbox providers (vendor-reported; no workload or duration details in the post).

The pitch targets long-lived, mostly idle agents rather than burst compute, where per-sandbox cost dominates at fleet scale.

For builders: compare managed sandbox vendors on idle cost and cold-start behavior for your agent's duty cycle, not only on peak-compute pricing. Validate the isolation model before moving credentials in.
