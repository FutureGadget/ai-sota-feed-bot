**Sandy**, from Kontext Security, is a sandbox for AI coding agents that adds **monitoring and policy controls** on top of isolation.

It is the same install-and-go shape as Workdir or Agent-run, plus the ongoing-visibility half of the stack: seeing what the agent did inside the box, not just containing it.
