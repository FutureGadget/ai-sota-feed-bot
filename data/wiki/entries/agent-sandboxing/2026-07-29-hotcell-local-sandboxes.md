**Hotcell** (Apache-2.0) lets you create, pause, and manage sandboxes on a laptop, a Linux VM, or bare metal. Its author benchmarked it against the ComputeSDK sandbox benchmarks.

Lifecycle controls such as pause and resume, previously a hosted-platform feature, are now available for self-run sandboxes.
