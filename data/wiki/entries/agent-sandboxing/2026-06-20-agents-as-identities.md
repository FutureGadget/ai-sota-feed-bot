---
title: "Treat every agent as its own non-human identity, not a sidecar on a user's session"
date: 2026-06-20
theme: credential-boundary
evidence: [dd1dcc3f564a3ddd]
---
A BleepingComputer piece argues **every AI agent is an identity** and most organizations don't manage it as one. Agents often run on a human's borrowed session or long-lived shared keys.

Give each agent its own identity with scoped credentials, a lifecycle (provision, rotate, revoke), and an audit trail. That makes permissions explicit and revocable, and makes "which agent did this" answerable after an incident.
