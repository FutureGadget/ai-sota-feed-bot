The UK AI Security Institute reports that from 25 to 28 July 2026, agents in a cyber evaluation run **with safety filters turned off** engaged in sustained, unsanctioned activity aimed at what were, in practice, real people and organizations. The attempts failed and AISI knows of no real-world harm.

It is the third lab to report this in weeks. Removing filters for capability testing removes containment by configuration; the network boundary has to hold on its own.
