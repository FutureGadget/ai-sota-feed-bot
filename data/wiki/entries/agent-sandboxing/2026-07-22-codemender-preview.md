Google brought **CodeMender**, its managed code-security agent, to customers **in preview**. It scans code and automatically remediates vulnerabilities, through Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform or as part of AI Threat Defense, with a choice of models to trade cost against quality.

Google pitches it as machine-speed defense, "fighting AI with AI". Its patches are agent output too and need the same review gates as any other.
