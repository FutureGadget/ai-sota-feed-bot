**GPT-Red** is OpenAI's automated red-teaming system. It uses **self-play** to improve safety, alignment, and prompt-injection robustness.

Red-teaming shifts from a point-in-time external exercise to an ongoing loop that keeps finding gaps in the other layers. Teams without such a system still need recurring adversarial tests, not a one-off audit.
