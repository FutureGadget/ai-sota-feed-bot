HIVE is an evaluation harness for **post-hallucination reasoning**: what happens after hallucinated content enters a vision-language model's context. Across nine tasks and nine models, hallucinated captions **often improved accuracy on vision-language tasks** by broadening semantic coverage, while text-only tasks showed limited or unstable effects.

"Does the agent hallucinate" is the wrong single-axis question. What matters is whether a given hallucination widens useful context or misleads the next reasoning step.
