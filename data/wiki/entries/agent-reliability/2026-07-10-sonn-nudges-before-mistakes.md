Sonn is a persistent reasoning layer that watches a coding agent's session live and injects a nudge the moment a past decision becomes relevant again, such as **a previously rejected approach or a settled architecture choice**.

It moves reliability work upstream: from checking the output after the fact to steering the decision before the agent makes it. The claim comes from a Show HN launch, with no published accuracy numbers.
