Anthropic's analysis of roughly **400,000 Claude Code sessions** splits responsibility along the plan/execute line: people make about **70% of planning decisions** but only **20% of execution decisions**. Expert users trigger about twice the actions per prompt that novices do (12 vs. 5) and five times the output (3,200 vs. 600 words).

Reliability today leans on where humans still hold the pen, which is planning rather than unattended execution. Removing that human checkpoint removes part of the reliability you are currently getting for free.
