Tech Times coverage puts Grok 4.5's coding-agent cost **roughly 80% below** a comparable frontier setup, at near-frontier speed and accuracy, but with the **hallucination rate roughly doubling** as accuracy rose. The figures are from press coverage, not an independent benchmark.

A cheaper, faster model is not a free swap. The saving only holds if the harness adds verification to catch the extra hallucinations; see [agent cost](/topic/agent-cost) for the cost side.
