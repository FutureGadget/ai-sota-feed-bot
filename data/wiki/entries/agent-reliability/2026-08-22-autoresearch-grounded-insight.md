AutoResearch is a two-stage system that links idea generation to idea execution so autonomous research workflows stay scientifically grounded. Its premise, "**insight in, hallucination out**", is that automation alone does not keep a long research run rigorous.

For long-running agents, grounding has to be a design target of the workflow, checked through experiments, rather than assumed from automation.
