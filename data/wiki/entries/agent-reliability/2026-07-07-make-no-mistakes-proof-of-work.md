Make No Mistakes, a Show HN project, is built around one rule: an AI coding agent must **prove its work** with verifiable evidence of completion instead of the harness trusting the agent's own "done" signal.

It is a practitioner-side instance of the same idea the research work on this page converges on. False completion is a known failure mode, so the completion claim itself has to be checked.
