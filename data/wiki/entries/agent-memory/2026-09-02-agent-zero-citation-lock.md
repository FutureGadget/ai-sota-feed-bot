**Agent Zero Memory** splits a user's history into three parallel systems: an episodic timeline of what changed when, an entity-event knowledge graph, and a semantic store of durable facts. It enforces a **citation lock**: an answer may cite only evidence it actually retrieved, so the system abstains instead of guessing.

Provenance is built into the retrieval contract rather than checked after the fact, a structural defense against fabrication.
