A reproducible benchmark shows agent-memory systems **readily admit poisoned facts**: adversarial or wrong entries that are written once and then retrieved as trusted context on every later turn.

Persistent memory is a persistent attack surface. Write-time validation and provenance become part of the memory-engineering job, not just recall quality. See [prompt injection](/topic/prompt-injection).
