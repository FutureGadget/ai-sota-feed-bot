**Memctl** gives a coding agent's persistent instruction files (`CLAUDE.md`, `AGENTS.md`) **versioning, diffing, and rollback**.

It is a direct answer to catastrophic remembering: those files accumulate redundant, conflicting guidance, and without history there is no way to undo a bad edit.
