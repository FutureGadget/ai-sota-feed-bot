A practitioner write-up surveys **agent memory systems and knowledge graphs** through four open projects: Letta, Mem0, Graphiti, and Cognee.

For teams choosing a memory layer, these four are the usual starting shortlist, and they differ mainly in whether memory is organized as a store of facts or as a knowledge graph.
