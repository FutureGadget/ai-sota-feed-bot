**CABLE** names an *evidence-reachability* failure: even when a fact was stored correctly, later steps may not recover it through a bounded memory interface. Similarity retrieval handles topical recall but misses earlier plans or motivations that are semantically distant from the events they explain.

It adds antecedent-based links and expansion to reach that evidence. The interface, not just the store, limits what an agent can remember.
