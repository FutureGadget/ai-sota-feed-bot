**MemTrapBench** targets memory-induced cognitive traps: even faithfully recorded, semantically relevant memories can **distort reasoning or beliefs** and degrade the current task.

Most memory benchmarks stop at whether information was extracted, stored, and retrieved correctly. Retaining information is necessary but not sufficient; how memory reshapes reasoning needs its own score.
