**UTILMEM** (1,717 instances across five domains) scores *memory utilization*: reasoning over dense histories, catching implicitly relevant memories, synthesizing evidence across sessions, and resisting similar-looking distractors.

Strong scores on factual-recall benchmarks **don't reliably predict utilization**. Even when a system retrieves the right evidence, it often fails to integrate it or is misled by a plausible distractor. Retrieval alone is insufficient, and now it is separately measurable.
