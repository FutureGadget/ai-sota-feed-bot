"Towards Root Memories" shows that memory retrieval based on semantic similarity **misses memories that are logically critical but share little wording** with the query. It builds IMLogic, a benchmark for implicit logical memory retrieval in long dialogues, and proposes *root memory*, a structured, decision-preserving representation of reusable personal facts.

The recall step has to reason over what is stored, not just embed and rank. See [vector/graph retrieval](/topic/vector-kb).
