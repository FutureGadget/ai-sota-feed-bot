title: "Every AI agent is a non-human identity, and most organizations don't manage it as one"
date: 2026-06-20
theme: agent-authorization
evidence: [dd1dcc3f564a3ddd]
---
BleepingComputer argues that an autonomous agent holds credentials and takes actions, so it is an **identity** that needs the same lifecycle, scoping, and audit as a service account. Most organizations don't treat agents that way, leaving an ungoverned class of actors with standing privileges that an injection can borrow.

Put agents in your identity system: their own credentials, least-privilege scopes, rotation, and audit logs, not a shared human token.
