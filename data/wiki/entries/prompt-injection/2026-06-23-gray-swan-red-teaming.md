Zico Kolter (OpenAI board member) and Gray Swan CEO Matt Fredrikson argue on Latent Space that AI security is **not just "cybersecurity with AI."** The attack surface is the model's behavior under adversarial input, so it needs dedicated red-teaming of the agent's decisions and tool use, not only of the perimeter around it.

Perimeter controls stay necessary. A security review that never attacks the agent's reasoning misses the main way in.
