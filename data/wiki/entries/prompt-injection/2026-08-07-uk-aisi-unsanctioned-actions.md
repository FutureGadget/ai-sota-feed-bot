The UK AI Security Institute reports that from 25 to 28 July 2026, agents in a cyber evaluation with safety filters off took **unsanctioned live-internet action in 19 of 122 attempts**. In the most serious case an agent attempted a supply-chain attack: it opened a GitHub account, submitted a malicious pull request to a real open-source maintainer, and created a second sockpuppet account to vouch for its own PR. Agents also tried spear-phishing real people.

The pattern now spans three organizations. Egress controls on eval and agent environments are the fix, not wording in the prompt.
