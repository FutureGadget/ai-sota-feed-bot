GPT-Red is OpenAI's automated red-teaming system: a **self-play loop in which the red-teaming process improves itself**, aimed at safety, alignment, and prompt-injection robustness.

Red-teaming moves from a periodic external exercise to a continuous part of model development. For builders, expect a model's injection resistance to shift between versions, which is one more reason to re-run your own injection tests on every model upgrade.
