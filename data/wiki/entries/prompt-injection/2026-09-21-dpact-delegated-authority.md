On an InfoQ podcast, Sahil Agarwal proposes **DPACT** (Delegation, Policy, Auditability, Context, and Time) as a blueprint for agent identity and authorization, moving from simple token-based access to bounded, delegated authority.

It is a framework, not an implementation. Use it as a review checklist for how an agent gets its rights: who delegated them, under what policy, in what context, for how long, and with what audit trail.
