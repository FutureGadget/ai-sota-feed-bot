The Open Secure AI Alliance, now **more than 120 organizations**, is developing guidelines to strengthen agentic-AI cybersecurity. As Black Hat opened, the Linux Foundation shared a Request for Comments on SAFE (Shared AI Findings Exchange), a proposed set of guidelines for cybersecurity transparency.

Cross-vendor disclosure norms are forming next to provider-level controls. A shared format for findings would make incident reports such as the Hugging Face breach easier for other teams to act on.
