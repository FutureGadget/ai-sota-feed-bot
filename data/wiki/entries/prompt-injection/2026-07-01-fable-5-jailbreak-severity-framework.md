Anthropic redeployed Claude Fable 5 from July 1, after export controls lifted, with updated cybersecurity safeguards and a new industry jailbreak framework. A follow-up spells out **what its cyber classifiers do and don't block** and publishes a first draft of a jailbreak *severity* framework, which grades how bad a successful jailbreak is rather than only detecting one.

Jailbreak resistance is now a versioned release concern. Severity grading lets a provider triage bypasses instead of treating each one as equally urgent.
