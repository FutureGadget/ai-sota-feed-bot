Security reporting describes **prompt injection used defensively** to thwart the AI hacking agents attackers deploy.

The technique works on any LLM-driven actor that reads untrusted content, offensive tooling included. The role confusion that endangers your agents also limits an attacker's, so content an attacking agent will read becomes a place to plant defenses.
