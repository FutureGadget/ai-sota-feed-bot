Amazon says attackers running Cairn, an open-source AI penetration-testing framework, gave agents a list of IP addresses and the goal "obtain shell access." With no further human input, the agents found and exploited a zero-day in an open-source medical-imaging AI application on a university server.

For builders: AI applications you self-host are now in scope for autonomous discovery, not just known-CVE scanning. Shorten the patch window on internet-facing inference and agent tooling, and keep them off the open internet where you can.
