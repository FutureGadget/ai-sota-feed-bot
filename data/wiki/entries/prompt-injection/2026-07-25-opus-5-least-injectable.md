Boris Cherny pointed to a section of the Opus 5 system card (page 73): across prompt-injection evals and red-teaming, **Opus 5 is very hard to prompt inject successfully**. He called that more exciting than the model's other eval scores.

Model-level resistance is improving release over release, but this is the vendor's own measurement. It lowers the base rate of successful injections; it does not remove the need for authorization limits around the agent.
