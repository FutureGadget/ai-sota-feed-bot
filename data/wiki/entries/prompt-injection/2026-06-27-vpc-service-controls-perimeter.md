Google Cloud recommends **VPC Service Controls** as a network-level perimeter for agentic workloads. Agents connect across tools and datasets, so a perimeter limits which services and data they can reach, whatever an instruction tells them to do.

A network boundary caps the blast radius of a hijacked agent, including where it can send data. It complements per-tool authorization rather than replacing it.
