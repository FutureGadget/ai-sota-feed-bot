OpenAI named **GPT-5.6-Cyber** as the model behind Daybreak Red, for authorized vulnerability research, exploit validation, and security testing. Access is gated to approved Daybreak partners who deliver governed, authorized services to customers, and Daybreak capabilities are now available through Amazon Bedrock.

The authorization question widens: not only which agent holds credentials to your systems, but who may use a frontier offensive-security model at all, and through what channel.
