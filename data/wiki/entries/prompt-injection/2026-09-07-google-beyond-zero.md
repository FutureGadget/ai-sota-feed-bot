title: "Google's Beyond Zero moves Zero Trust authorization down to each resource and action"
date: 2026-09-07
theme: agent-authorization
evidence: [c8a981c019159c5b]
---
Google's Beyond Zero updates the BeyondCorp Zero Trust model, which assumed accessors are human, act at human speed, and that the application is the right trust boundary. All three break for autonomous agents. Beyond Zero moves access decisions to **individual resources and actions**, combining static authorization with dynamic, AI-driven risk decisions for machine-speed enforcement on humans and agents alike.

Continuous per-action checks replace a standing session grant, so an injected instruction has to clear authorization on every action instead of riding an open session.
