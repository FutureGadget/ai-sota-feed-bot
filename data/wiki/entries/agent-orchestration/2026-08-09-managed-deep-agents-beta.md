**Managed Deep Agents** entered public beta: the Deep Agents harness deployed to a managed LangSmith runtime with **durable execution, memory, sandboxes, channels, and evals**.

The framework vendor now ships the production infrastructure around its own harness instead of leaving deployment to whichever platform (Bedrock, Foundry) an adopter picks. Weigh the convenience against how much of your estate then depends on one vendor's runtime.
