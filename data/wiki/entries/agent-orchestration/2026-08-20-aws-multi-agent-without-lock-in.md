The second post in AWS's multi-agent series starts from the fact that an organization running many agentic systems already lives in a **"multi-everything" environment** of several frameworks, models, and providers at once. It argues for patterns that keep those systems composable as that mix changes.

This is the counterweight to the managed-runtime launches on this page: harness choices that teams make independently become expensive to unwind once the systems have to scale together.
