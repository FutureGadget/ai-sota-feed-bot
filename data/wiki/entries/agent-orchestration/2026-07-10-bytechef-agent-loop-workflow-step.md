Bytechef, an open-source workflow automation platform, embeds a full agent loop (model call, tool invocation, observation, next-step decision) as a **drag-and-drop workflow step** that can trigger or be triggered by ordinary steps. Agent decisions, tool calls, and human approvals share one audit trail.

Agents and workflows stop being separate layers. It is the "put coordination in ordinary code" pattern, expressed as a visual builder.
