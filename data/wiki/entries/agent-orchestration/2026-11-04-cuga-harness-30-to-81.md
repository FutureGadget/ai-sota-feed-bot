title: "Swapping the harness moved one open-weight model from 30% to 81% task completion"
date: 2026-11-04
theme: harness-choice
evidence: [b05fd13d79e76b34]
---
IBM Research gave CUGA, Hermes, and Deep Agents the same open-weight model on 90 AppWorld tasks. Completion ranged from 30% to 81% by harness alone (self-run, vendor-authored). The post cites another analysis where one model went from 23% to 52% across harnesses.

For builders: with nearly 200 harnesses on the market, benchmark two or three on your own tasks before choosing. CUGA's pitch also adds harness-enforced rules and in-boundary deployment, which this benchmark does not test.
