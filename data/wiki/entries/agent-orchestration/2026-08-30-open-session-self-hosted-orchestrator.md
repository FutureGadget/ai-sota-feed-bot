**Open Session** is a self-hosted, cloud-based agent orchestrator that works with any model, or several at once. It began as an internal app at Tella, where the whole team uses it for product development, customer support, operations, and data analysis.

Unlike most launches in this wave, it was **dogfooded in production before release**. Its stated motivation was that existing tools lacked adaptability.
