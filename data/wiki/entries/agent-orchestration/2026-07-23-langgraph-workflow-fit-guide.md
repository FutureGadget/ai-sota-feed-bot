A practitioner guide positions LangGraph by workflow fit, not as a universal default. Three executable recipes, **SQL analytics with repair loops, RAG with evidence gating, and human-in-the-loop policy review with interrupt and checkpoint recovery**, show where typed state, conditional routing, retries, interrupts, checkpoints, and traces pay off.

For simpler jobs it recommends ReAct-style loops, schema-first tools, or DSPy. Pick the framework by how stateful and long-running the process is.
