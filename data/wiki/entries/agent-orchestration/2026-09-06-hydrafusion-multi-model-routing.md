GitHub's **Project HydraFusion** routes each step of a coding workflow to the model that step needs. In **controlled offline evaluations**, its selective workflows matched or exceeded an Opus 5 baseline while reducing estimated workflow cost. It ships as a research preview in GitHub Copilot.

Heterogeneous-model collaboration lands inside a tool engineers already use daily. The numbers are vendor-reported and offline; see [agent cost](/topic/agent-cost) for the cost side.
