# Story permalink UX contract

Story permalinks answer one question: **is this source worth opening, and where
does it fit?** They are concise source briefs, not article mirrors.

## Reading order

1. **Source identity** — headline, publisher, and publication date.
2. **In brief** — the deterministic short summary or release highlights.
3. **Supporting context** — editorial why-it-matters and storyline continuity,
   when available.
4. **Feed lens** — relevant topic labels without ranking diagnostics.
5. **Continue reading** — the original source is the primary action; Live feed
   and the matching daily brief are secondary.
6. **Earlier in this thread** — precise related history, not generic
   recommendations.

The page must never reproduce the full RSS/article body. Thin historical pages
still explain their source and direct the reader outward.

## Visual behavior

Story pages use the shared instrument-paper palette, condensed display
typography, and monospace provenance labels. Their signature is the
source-dossier header and a clear source exit gate. Supporting context uses a
two-column ruled layout on wide screens and stacks on mobile.

Light/dark themes, visible keyboard focus, 44 px actions, and reduced-motion
behavior follow the site-wide accessibility contract.
